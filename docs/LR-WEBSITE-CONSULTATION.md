# Leicestershire & Rutland — Public Website Consultation Concepts

**Date:** 8 October 2026. Proposed identity: *Leicestershire & Rutland Widows Sons Masonic Bikers Association*; display shorthand: *Widows Sons L&R*. All options are proposals subject to chapter sign-off.

## Option A: Heritage & Road (recommended initial direction)
- Dark charcoal (#111820), brushed brass-gold (#C49A59), off-white (#F4F0E8).
- High-quality photography of local roads, motorcycles, and charity activities. Strong typography and sober presentation.
- Hero: **Leicestershire & Rutland Widows Sons**; secondary line: **Motorcycling · Friendship · Charity**.
- Clear calls to action: **See Our Rides**, **Our Charity Work**, **Member Login**.
- Emphasis on trust, heritage, purposeful riding; no theatrical skull artwork or unapproved insignia.

## Option B: Modern Touring
- Slate, soft ivory, muted red and clear map/route panels.
- Homepage foregrounds next ride, recent ride photos, GPX routes and calendar.
- Designed for easy use on a phone in poor weather (stationary-use controls only).

## Option C: Community & Charity
- Deep green, cream, muted gold; people-led photographs.
- Featured charity outcomes, volunteering, family and associate activities, public events.
- Prominent Almoner contact only in the private clubhouse, not confidential details on the public site.

## Shared page structure
- `/` Home, `/about` About L&R, `/rides` Public events, `/charity` Charity and achievements, `/shop` Merchandise, `/news` News, `/gallery` Approved photos, `/contact` Contact.
- `/members` Authenticated clubhouse; `/admin` permission-gated back office, shared identity and API.
- Public pages should not leak names, locations, chapter correspondence or emergency contacts without consent.

Check domain availability with a registrar and confirm permissions to use the name and any chapter/national logo before purchase, print or publication.

## Website implementation
Keep within **the existing public repository** `lurcheous73/widows-sons`. Public pages, members clubhouse, administrator portal, mobile apps and shared multi-organisation backend are parts of **one project**, not separate repositories.

Use accessible UK-English copy, high contrast, 44px-or-larger touch targets, alt text, keyboard access, consent-aware analytics, privacy notices and spam-resistant contact handling. Do not claim live member login, merchandise checkout, charity donations or safety services until functioning, secure and approved.

## Assets and approvals required
Approved chapter name and marks; permission for road/charity photographs; official point of contact; charity beneficiary identity and payment arrangements; brand vote; choice and ownership of domain; public chapter history and membership description.
